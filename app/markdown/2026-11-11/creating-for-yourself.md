# Creating for yourself

Today marks the 7th anniversary of starting this blog. If you've been following along for a while: *thank you!* 😊

After observing [Volunteer Responsibility Amnesty Day](https://sethmlarson.dev/observing-volunteer-amnesty-day) in 2025
and stopping most of my volunteer OSS contributions, I really leaned hard into
using my creative leisure time "for me". This has helped protect
my flame from burn-out and focus on areas I'm excited about, like writing,
video-games (retro, modding, ...developing? 👀), and keeping my volunteer OSS focus on
mostly a single project: [truststore](https://github.com/sethmlarson/truststore/). This year was the first time [since 2016](https://github.com/urllib3/urllib3/graphs/contributors?from=1%2F1%2F2016&to=11%2F7%2F2026)
that I was uninvolved in the latest release of urllib3, which felt both deeply rewarding and
strange as I read the [v2.8.0 release notes](https://github.com/urllib3/urllib3/releases/tag/2.8.0).

## Writing

This year I [published 54 articles to my blog](https://mastodon.social/@sethmlarson/117554489864114380), an average of once per week across a year.
This wasn't a specific goal I had in mind, looking through the past year of articles has me
thinking "*oh, that was this year??*". Writing continued to be a focus of my role at the PSF, too, where I [wrote 14 articles](https://blog.python.org/authors/seth-larson/)
for [Python Insider](https://blog.python.org/authors/seth-larson/),
[5 for the PSF blog](https://pyfound.blogspot.com), and [2 for the Python Package Index blog](https://blog.pypi.org/author/sethmlarson/) (*that's a lot of yapping!*)

The piece I'm most proud of from this year is “[Food JPEGs in Super Smash Bros. and Kirby Air Riders](https://sethmlarson.dev/food-jpegs-in-super-smash-bros-and-kirby-air-riders)”.
This took *tons* of research, buying the Japanese Kirby Air Ride, [writing some custom software](https://sethmlarson.dev/cutting-spritesheets-like-cookies-with-python-and-pillow),
and I think this work really showed through in the end. This was my most-visited article of the year (mostly from the [Hacker News front page](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46516807)).

My under-dog favorite is “[Adding Animal Crossing events to your digital calendar](https://sethmlarson.dev/animal-crossing-calendar)” where
I got to deep-dive into how Animal Crossing events work, differences between regions and games, the Animal Crossing
decompilation project, and bought a physical Animal Crossing calendar too. I
learned about the ICS calendar format and which features are supported across
different platforms like iOS and Android.

## Gaming

Ever since 2023 I've been “in my feels” about gaming. I considered myself
a gamer, but I wasn't *actually playing games*. I set a goal to get back into
the hobby more seriously, and figure out how I can integrate play back
into my week. 2025 was successful already, and 2026 was even more so.

I ended up really experimenting this year by writing about
a slice of my experience for four of the games I completed. I don't
think I'll want to write about every game I play, but
thinking deeply about my feelings
while playing the game and *especially* the time between play sessions
has been an interesting lens to look through. *Maybe more to come here?*

* [“The Legend of Zelda: Link’s Awakening” respects your time](https://sethmlarson.dev/links-awakening)
